ABOUT ME

I am a developer focused on the intersection of cloud architecture and high-precision navigation. My recent work involves building EagleNav, a pedestrian-centric platform designed to solve the "last 100 feet" of navigation where traditional GPS often fails.

I specialize in creating efficient, scalable mapping solutions by:

  • Architecting Hybrid Systems: Using Dockerized Valhalla engines and optimizing data delivery via Cloudflare and GCP to keep infrastructure high-performing and cost-effective.
  • Precision Navigation: Implementing sensor fusion and custom topological logic to provide intelligent, sub-meter accurate turn-by-turn guidance.
  • Full-Stack Development: Crafting beautiful, user-friendly React interfaces that transform complex spatial data into seamless, accessible experiences.

I am passionate about building robust "digital twins" of the physical world that provide real-world utility and exceptional functionality.
